Retail buyers want move-in ready. Their lenders want the house to pass an appraisal and inspection. If your home has a failing roof, foundation cracks, old wiring, water damage, mold, or simply hasn't been updated since it was built, a traditional sale often means fixing it first or watching deals fall apart.

## What "as-is" actually means with me

- **No repairs before closing.** None. Not even cleaning.
- **No inspection contingencies.** I do my own walkthrough and take the risk from there.
- **No repair credits after the fact.** The number I give you is the number.
- **I fix houses for a living.** Repairs that scare off a family buyer are just line items on my budget.

## What I need from you

A quick walkthrough (in person or on video) and honest answers about what you know is wrong. That's it. You'll get a straight offer, usually within 48 hours.
