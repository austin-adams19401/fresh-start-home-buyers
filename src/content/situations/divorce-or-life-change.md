When a marriage ends or life turns a corner, the house is often the biggest thing that has to be dealt with, and the hardest to agree on. Listing it means months of showings, repair negotiations, and one more thing to argue about.

## Why a direct sale can help

- **One number, one date.** A single cash offer and a closing date you both agree to. No waiting on buyer financing that might fall through.
- **No repairs, no staging, no strangers walking through.** I buy the house as it is.
- **Both parties treated fairly.** I work with both of you (and your attorneys, if you have them) and keep everyone informed.
- **Move on sooner.** Most people tell me the relief of having it done is worth more than squeezing out the last few percent.

## Other life changes

The same applies to a job loss, a health change, a move to care for family, or a home that just doesn't fit anymore. If you need it handled simply and on your timeline, reach out and we'll figure out the best route, even if that route isn't selling to me.
