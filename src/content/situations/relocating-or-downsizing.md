A job transfer, a move closer to grandkids, or a house that is just too big now. Whatever the reason, moving is stressful enough without juggling showings, open houses, and a buyer's financing timeline that might not line up with yours.

## Why sellers on a deadline call me

- **You pick the closing date.** Ten days or ninety. If you need to close after your move, that works too.
- **Stay after closing if you need to.** A short rent-back after closing is often possible so you aren't moving twice.
- **No prep work.** Pack what you want and leave the rest.
- **Certainty.** No appraisal, no loan approval, no buyer backing out the week before your movers arrive.

## When listing might be better

If your home is updated, you have a couple of months of runway, and you'd rather maximize price than simplicity, a listing can net more. I'll say so, and I can connect you with an agent I trust. See [list with an agent](/list-with-an-agent/).
