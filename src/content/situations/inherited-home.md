Inheriting a house usually comes with a lot more than a set of keys. There is a mortgage or taxes that keep coming due, a lifetime of belongings inside, repairs nobody has gotten to, and often several family members with different opinions about what to do.

## How I can help

- **Buy it exactly as it sits.** Leave whatever you don't want. I handle the cleanout after closing.
- **Work around probate.** If the estate is still in probate, I can write the offer now and close when the court allows. If it is already through, we can move quickly.
- **Keep the family on the same page.** One buyer, one clear offer, one closing date. No showings, no back-and-forth with agents, no repair negotiations.
- **Explain the other routes too.** If the house is in good shape and the family isn't in a hurry, listing it might net more. I'll tell you that.

## What people usually ask

**Do we need to clean it out first?** No. Take the things that matter and leave the rest.

**What if there are still tenants or a family member living there?** That's workable. Tell me the situation and we'll figure out the timeline together.

**What about the mortgage?** If there is a loan on the house, it gets paid off at closing like any other sale. If the estate is behind on payments, sooner is better. Call me before it becomes a foreclosure problem.
